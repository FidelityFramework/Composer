# Parallel Nanopass Architecture

CCS/Baker owns semantic nanopasses. Parallel execution preserves their source
contracts, ordered joint premises and intermediate rewrite record. Alex consumes
an immutable settled publication through the actual Huet occurrence and passive
Element/Pattern/Witness composition.

The January 27 witness-per-pass executor and accumulator-overlay proposal is
retired. Its pseudocode, migration checklist and unsupported independence and
speed claims have been removed. That proposal supplies no implementation or
acceptance evidence.

## Source-owned work and publication

Baker ingredients and recipes elaborate source structure. Owning analyses settle
types, dimensions, ranges, demand, captures, layout, residence, declaration/ABI
facts and proof premises. Work can be grouped only when its complete read,
write, rewiring and publication dependencies justify that grouping. Relevant
absence facts and alternative supports remain part of the dependency record.

A structural subtree or disjoint node category does not establish independence.
Cross-scope bindings, shared storage, effects and ordered joint participants can
connect work outside that subtree. Resource capacity, convergence and semantic
equivalence need their own premises; pairwise independence or a coloring result
cannot discharge them.

Fan-out/fold-in preserves snapshot, source, occurrence, rule and participant
identities. A changed or withdrawn premise invalidates the dependent result.
Superseded work cannot publish facts, proofs or artifacts into a newer accepted
generation. Full and pruned views retain resolvable history without treating
retired participants as current proof premises or executable roots.

## Passive regional witnessing

CCS/Baker determines a region's semantic readiness and publishes immutable
codata with its admitted structure, declaration scope, typed operands and
complete correspondence. Alex navigates that publication at its actual Huet
focus and composes the supplied physical form. It does not query hyperedges to
derive facts, discover dependency regions, repair missing premises, or run a
second semantic worklist.

Physical SSA identities derive from settled graph roles and block arguments.
Emission bookkeeping supplies no semantic authority. Sharing Elements or
Patterns alone establishes neither concurrent safety nor associative output
merging. Source-authorized scoped witnessing must preserve operand availability,
declaration and region order, actual environment identity and artifact
correspondence.

The backend owns target-specific transformations, segmented objects and their
realization checks. All custom MLIR plugins and compatibility dependencies are
retired. No middle-end MLIR transform replaces source-owned settlement.

## Acceptance

Compare selective settlement with a fresh check of the same source, declarations
and rules, including diagnostics and retracted support. Check realized artifact
and native behavior equivalence separately. Exercise changed joint premises,
new lookup alternatives, recursive dependencies, live/dead transitions, segment
split/merge and stale-worker rejection.

Measure recomputation and latency only after those correctness gates. A .NET
compiler scheduling mechanism does not define Clef runtime async or actor
semantics. Parallel test execution, compiler parallelism and parallel execution
inside a compiled program retain separate oracles and evidence.
